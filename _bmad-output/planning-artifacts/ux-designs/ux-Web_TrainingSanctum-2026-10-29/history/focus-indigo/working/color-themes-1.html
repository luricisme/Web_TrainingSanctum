Training Sanctum — Color Theme Variations

Five directions on the locked visual brief: dark navy/charcoal base with a single deep-red pop accent in dark mode; sage-green canvas with floating white cards in light mode (Spy x Family site register). Each variation shows the flashcard review screen (dark) and the folder/library list (light) side by side, with exact hex tokens documented below and in CSS comments.
